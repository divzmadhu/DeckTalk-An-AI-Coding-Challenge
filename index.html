    Help & About

    DeckTalk helps you practise useful English-to-Malayalam travel words and phrases.

    Using the flashcards

    • Choose a deck or category to browse, or search for cards across all decks.
    • Select a card to reveal its Malayalam translation and transliteration.
    • Use Previous and Next to move between cards, or Shuffle to change their order. The left and right arrow keys also navigate cards.

    Managing your cards

    Open Menu and choose Manage Decks to create, rename, or delete decks, or choose Manage Cards to add, edit, or remove cards.

    Your data

    Your decks and cards are saved in this browser. They do not sync to other devices.
